About CV Raman Club

Named after the legendary Nobel laureate Sir C.V. Raman, this club is dedicated to igniting curiosity and deep scientific exploration among engineering students. We focus on bridging theoretical physics with practical applications through rigorous experimentation and collaborative projects.

Through research seminars, rocketry workshops, science exhibitions, and expert mentorship, members develop critical problem-solving skills and an enduring passion for scientific discovery.
