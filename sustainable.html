About Sustainable Agriculture Club

The Sustainable Agriculture Club serves as an ecological incubator dedicated to promoting organic farming, water conservation, precision agritech, and sustainable food systems within the academic community.

Through hands-on vermicomposting labs, hydroponic setups, soil testing workshops, and local farmer outreach programs, members champion green agricultural practices that protect the planet and ensure long-term food security.

COVER STORY • PILLAR 01

Organic Farming & Composting

Intensive hands-on training in chemical-free cultivation, natural pest repellents, and soil regeneration.

  • Training in organic farming methods & vermicomposting
  • Bio-fertilizers & zero-budget natural farming practices
INNOVATION • PILLAR 02

Hydroponics & Precision Agritech

Exploring modern smart-farming techniques and automated resource management.

  • Soilless hydroponics & drip irrigation workshops
  • IoT sensors & smart greenhouse management
PILLAR 03

Soil Health & Water Conservation

  • Soil testing camps & watershed management
  • Rainwater harvesting & crop rotation strategies
PILLAR 04

Botanical Garden & Nursery

  • Maintaining campus botanical gardens
  • Medicinal plant nurseries, seed banks & terrace farming
PILLAR 05

Farmers Outreach & Workshops

  • Awareness workshops for local farmers
  • Sustainable crop yields & biological pest control
PILLAR 06

Agri-Innovation & Research

  • Student-led research in climate-resilient crops
