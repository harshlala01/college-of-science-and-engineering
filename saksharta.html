About Saksharta Club

Saksharta Club (Electoral Literacy Club) at CSE Jhansi is dedicated to instilling democratic values and awareness among the student community. We focus on driving continuous voter registration, conducting ethical voting awareness, and educating young citizens on electoral procedures.

Through systematic voter education initiatives, SVEEP activities, and community outreach campaigns, the club equips young voters with legal literacy and civic responsibilities essential for robust democratic governance.

ACTIVITY HIGHLIGHTS

Empowering Future Achievers

Hands-on participation in electoral awareness drives, EVM/VVPAT workshops, interactive youth debates, and community literacy outreach.

DOMAIN AREA Social & Media
AFFILIATION SAC • CSE Jhansi

Key Pillars & Initiatives

A structured initiative for voter literacy, active participation, and democratic awareness.

Voter Registration Drives

  • Organising help desks for easy registration
  • Assisting first-time voters with online forms
  • Encouraging eligible young citizens to participate

Voter Awareness Campaigns (SVEEP)

  • Conducting interactive electoral workshops
  • Organising awareness rallies across campus
  • Promoting informed, ethical voting principles

EVM & VVPAT Demonstrations

  • Organizing hands-on machine demonstrations
  • Explaining VVPAT role in transparent elections
  • Helping students verify cast votes accurately

Competitions & Workshops

  • Hosting poster making competitions on civic duty
  • Organising slogan writing & creative painting
  • Conducting essay writing, speech and debate

Specialized Days

  • Celebrating National Voters' Day - January 25
  • Observing Constitution Day - November 26
  • Organising voter pledges and interactive quizzes

Literacy Outreach

  • Extending electoral literacy beyond campus
  • Conducting Chunav Pathshala in nearby areas
  • Helping communities understand voting rights
