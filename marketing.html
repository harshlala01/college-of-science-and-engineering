About Marketing Club

The Marketing Club provides a vibrant ecosystem for students to bridge theoretical marketing frameworks with practical campaign design and consumer engagement. We nurture strategic thinking and creative execution.

Through Ad-Mad shows, live campus sales bazaars, business plan competitions, and market research simulations, members learn to position products, decode buyer psychology, and drive commercial success.

Hands-on participation in live marketing simulations, campus trade fairs, and corporate pitch sessions.

Key Pillars & Initiatives

A dynamic, campaign-style roadmap designed to master marketing strategy and brand building.

Creative & Advertising Activities

  • Ad-Mad Shows showcasing funny or persuasive live commercials
  • Poster & Brand Design contests for creative visual storytelling
  • "Product Twist" challenges re-branding ordinary household items

Strategy & Simulation Games

  • Interactive Marketing Simulations for market share conquest
  • Business Plan Contests for new product launches
  • Case Challenges solving real corporate marketing dilemmas
  • Brand & Logo Quizzes evaluating industry trivia

Real-World Marketing Exposure

  • Campus Bazaar / Live Sales stalls testing real negotiation
  • Consumer Research Studies decoding student purchasing trends
  • Guest Lectures & Webinars hosted by CMOs and brand leads
  • Marketing Journals & Newsletters highlighting industry trends

Networking & Professional Growth

  • Workshops & Seminars on SEO, digital ads, and social media
  • Industrial Visits to advertising agencies and corporate hubs
  • Role-Playing Activities mastering high-stakes sales pitching
