About ABC Engineering & Technology Institute

ABC Engineering & Technology Institute is a premier technical institution committed to delivering world-class engineering education, fostering path-breaking research, and cultivating innovative leadership. Located in Jhansi, our 80-acre lush green campus provides an enriching academic environment supported by ultra-modern laboratories, smart classrooms, and active industry partnerships.

We focus on holistic education that combines strong theoretical fundamentals with hands-on practical execution, preparing our graduates to excel in global engineering and corporate environments.

Our Vision
