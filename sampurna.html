About Sampurna Club

Sampurna Club at the College of Science & Engineering (CSE), Jhansi is tailored to drive complete personality transformation. We focus on enhancing spoken English, mastering the art of storytelling, fine-tuning debate skills, and nurturing refined interpersonal etiquette.

Through structured mentorship and experiential learning initiatives, the club bridges academic knowledge with core life competencies, preparing young professionals to shine in competitive global environments with confidence and poise.

ACTIVITY HIGHLIGHTS

Empowering Future Achievers

Hands-on participation in public speaking workshops, confidence building drills, leadership grooming, and holistic wellness sessions.

DOMAIN AREA Social & Media
AFFILIATION SAC • CSE Jhansi

Key Pillars & Initiatives

A dynamic, multi-faceted approach to personal transformation, career acceleration, and holistic well-being.

Communication & Public Speaking

  • Training in spoken English, storytelling, debate, and seminars on professional etiquette.

Confidence & Soft Skills

  • Self-assessment quizzes, role-playing exercises, and workshops to eliminate stage fright.

Career Guidance & Leadership

  • Sessions on career acceleration, leadership grooming, teamwork exercises, and CV writing.

Technical & Modern Skills

  • Coding sessions, AI for beginners, and hackathons under initiatives like Project Sampoorna.

Social & Emotional Wellness

  • Meditation sessions (such as Sudarshan Kriya), yoga, self-defense workshops, and emotional intelligence webinars.

Cultural & Creative Activities

  • Music, singing, drama, and art workshops designed to reduce stress and enhance creativity.
