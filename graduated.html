Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech)

Our B.Tech program is designed to build strong technical foundations, foster innovation, and prepare students for successful careers in a rapidly evolving global environment.

Program Aims
  • Provide industry-relevant and future-ready curriculum
  • Develop problem-solving and critical thinking skills
  • Encourage innovation, research and entrepreneurship
  • Prepare students for global career opportunities
  • Build ethical, responsible and socially conscious engineers
Core Subjects
Engineering Mathematics
Computer Systems & Networks
Programming & Data Structures
AI, Data Science & Analytics
Digital Electronics
Communication Skills
Engineering Physics & Chemistry
Project-Based Learning
Design & Engineering Practice
