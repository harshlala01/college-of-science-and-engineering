About Research & Analytics Club

The Research & Analytics Club is a student-driven initiative committed to cultivating a robust data-centric mindset among engineers. We bridge academic concepts with real-world industry analytics using modern toolstacks like Python, R, SQL, Tableau, and Power BI.

Our focus extends to research methodologies, quantitative analysis, predictive modeling, and business intelligence, helping students transform raw data into actionable strategic insights.

ACTIVITY HIGHLIGHTS

Empowering Future Achievers

Hands-on participation in analytical hackathons, live industry projects, research paper publications, and expert mentorship.

Key Pillars & Initiatives

An integrated analytics framework built for skill transformation, research, and corporate exposure.

Workshops & Training

  • Conducting hands-on workshops on tools like Python, R, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, and Excel.

Case Study Competitions

  • Hosting competitions to solve business problems using analytical techniques, such as Fidati ca and Avenir.

Live Projects & Internships

  • Securing opportunities for students to work on real-time data projects with corporations.

Guest Lectures & Panel Discussions

  • Organizing sessions with industry experts, such as Colloquium or Ask Me Anything (AMA) sessions.

Knowledge Series & Research

  • Publishing newsletters, research blogs (e.g., Analytics Lens), and organizing journal clubs.

Annual Symposiums & Expos

  • Conducting events like annual research summits or analytics fests to showcase student talent.
