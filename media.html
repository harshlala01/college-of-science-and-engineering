About Media Club

The Media Club serves as the primary visual storytelling hub for the college, empowering students to master modern camera production, broadcast journalism, video editing, and digital media strategies.

Through hands-on short film production, podcasts, photojournalism, and studio field visits, members develop powerful creative and technical communication skills fit for the modern media landscape.

ACTIVITY HIGHLIGHTS

Empowering Future Achievers

Hands-on participation in live event coverage, studio production, and media house field trips.

Key Pillars & Initiatives

A dynamic, broadcast-inspired asymmetric framework designed for storytelling and production mastery.

Content Creation & Production

  • Short Film Making & Narrative Direction
  • Photography & Videography Workshops
  • Podcasting & Campus Radio Production
  • Journalism & Editorial Writing
  • Digital Marketing & Social Content Creation

Workshops & Skill Development

  • Technical Training: video editing, graphic design, audio mixing
  • Media Literacy: fact-checking & spotting fake news
  • Anchoring & Journalism: news reporting, scripting, RJ-ing

Events & Competitions

  • Media Fests: inter-collegiate festivals
  • Film Screenings: notable films and documentaries
  • Field Trips: visits to media houses, studios, and production sets

Publicity & Coverage

  • Event Promotion: designing posters, brochures, promotional videos
  • Social Media Campaigns: managing campaigns to highlight student achievements

Broadcast & Digital Media Excellence

  • Live Event Streaming & Multi-Camera Setup
  • Campus Press Release & Public Relations
  • Documentary Filmmaking on Local Community Stories
