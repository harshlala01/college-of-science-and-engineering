About Cinematic Club

The Cinematic Club at SRGI CSE Jhansi serves as an artistic haven for movie enthusiasts, aspiring directors, and media creators. We explore the profound impact of cinema as a cultural medium, bridging analytical critique with hands-on digital production.

Through engaging film screenings, masterclasses, screenplay labs, and collaborative short-film projects, we empower engineering scholars to articulate diverse narratives and master modern visual technologies.
