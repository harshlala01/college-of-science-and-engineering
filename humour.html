About Humour Club

The Humour Club provides a supportive and energetic environment for students to de-stress, build stage confidence, and sharpen their witty storytelling skills. We believe that laughter is essential for holistic mental well-being and creative thinking.

Through open mic comedy nights, laughter yoga sessions, satirical writing labs, and meme-making contests, members learn comedic timing, public speaking poise, and constructive satire.

ACTIVITY HIGHLIGHTS

Empowering Future Achievers

Hands-on participation in live stand-up sessions, improv battles, and peer comedic mentorship.
