About Athlete Club

The Athlete Club at SRGI CSE Jhansi is dedicated to nurturing robust physical health and mental resilience. We emphasize core athletic pillars including running, jumping, throwing, and specialized endurance training to build active, focused technocrats.

Through disciplined schedules, expert coaching, and inter-departmental tournaments, we instill teamwork, leadership, and competitive spirit, ensuring our engineering scholars thrive both inside laboratories and on the sports ground.
