About Social Media Club

The Social Media Club serves as the digital voice and creative powerhouse for the campus, providing students with hands-on experience in content strategy, visual storytelling, and platform analytics.

Through influencer masterclasses, campaign ideation, short-form video creation, and real-time social channel coverage, members learn to create compelling digital narratives that resonate across global audiences.

ACTIVITY HIGHLIGHTS

Empowering Future Achievers

Hands-on participation in live campus social management, digital campaigns, and creator masterclasses.

Key Pillars & Initiatives

A dynamic, trendy roadmap designed for content creation, growth hacking, and digital brand elevation.

Content Creation & Design

  • Graphic designing, reel creation, viral short-form video production, and storytelling across Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn.

Social Marketing & Growth

  • Campaign ideation, algorithm optimization, organic growth techniques, and digital brand promotion strategies.

Photography & Videography

  • Event coverage, campus photoshoot sessions, cinematic video shooting, and post-production video editing.

Analytics & Insights

  • Tracking performance metrics, reach, engagement rates, hashtag research, and audience behavioral insights.

Workshops & Masterclasses

  • Interactive sessions with digital influencers, content creators, and social media strategists.

Live Streaming & Coverage

  • Live streaming campus events, managing official college social handles, and driving student engagement.
