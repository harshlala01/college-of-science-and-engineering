About Aryabhatta Club

Named after the legendary Indian mathematician and astronomer Aryabhatta, our club serves as a vibrant nucleus for students passionate about advanced computation, mathematical structures, and logical reasoning within the Department of Computer Science & Engineering.

We bridge theoretical concepts and practical applications by organizing interactive workshops, collaborative hackathons, puzzle-solving challenges, and faculty-guided research sessions that cultivate analytical mastery essential for modern engineering careers.

Expert Faculty Mentorship Active Peer Learning

Empowering Future Achievers

Driven by dedicated faculty mentorship and dynamic student leadership, the club orchestrates targeted events designed to transform abstract mathematical theories into concrete software and computational solutions.
